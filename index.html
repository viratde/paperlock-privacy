Paperlock Privacy Policy

Paperlock is a privacy-first PDF toolkit that runs entirely on your device.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

Paperlock does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, or share any of your personal data or documents. Everything you scan, open, edit, protect, or sign stays on your device. We have no servers that receive your files, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Who we are

This policy applies to the Paperlock mobile application ("the App", "we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at info@iwebron.com.

Data we collect

None. Paperlock does not collect personal information. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no background data collection. We do not know who you are, and we never receive your documents.

How your documents are handled

  • All PDF and document processing — scanning, importing, merging, splitting, rotating, compressing, organizing, text extraction (OCR), searchable-PDF creation, password protection, watermarking, signing, and annotation — happens locally on your device.
  • Your files are never uploaded to us or to any third party by the App.
  • Files you create are saved to your device's storage, under your control. Deleting them in the App or your file manager removes them.

Device permissions

Paperlock requests only the permissions needed for features you choose to use, and uses them solely on-device:

  • Camera — only when you scan a document. Captured images are processed locally and are not sent anywhere.
  • Photos / Files / Storage — only to let you import images or PDFs you pick, and to save the files you create. We access only the files you explicitly select.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device's system settings.

On-device text recognition (OCR)

The "searchable PDF" and text-extraction features use on-device text recognition to read text from your pages. This runs locally on your device; your page images and recognized text are not transmitted to us.

Purchases

Paperlock offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Paperlock Pro") to unlock additional features. Payments are processed by Google Play Billing, not by us. We never see or store your payment card details. Google's handling of your purchase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The App stores only a local flag indicating whether Pro has been unlocked.

Analytics and advertising

Paperlock contains no third-party analytics SDKs and no advertising. We do not track your usage or build a profile of you.

Data sharing

Because we do not collect your data, we have nothing to sell or share. We do not share any personal data with third parties.

Children's privacy

Paperlock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy? Email info@iwebron.com.
